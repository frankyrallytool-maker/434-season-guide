Open the Season icon, tap ENTER, then teleport into your alliance hive.

Season→Enter→Alliance hive

Kill Boomers and Zombies to collect the season currency and resources you need for the opening progression.

Go to the bottom of the season tower and start building your Altar and Obelisk.

TIP Do this early so your seasonal progression is not delayed.

Shadow Call has been reset. Take advantage of the reset and complete it again.

Start your Ruins Tasks and read every ! carefully before choosing. Different tasks can offer different buffs for different troop types.

Choose wisely. Do not select a task only because it looks stronger — match the buff to the troops you actually use.
